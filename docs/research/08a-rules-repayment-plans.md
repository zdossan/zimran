# 08a — Repayment-plan rules for the calculation engine (verified against primary text)

Research date: 2026-11-14. Scope: federal Direct Loan repayment plans after P.L. 119-21 (OBBBA, which ED now calls the "Working Families Tax Cuts Act") and the RISE final rule.
Method: I pulled the regulation text from the eCFR versioner API as of 2026-11-01. eCFR shows Title 34 as up to date through 2026-11-12, and §685.209 was last amended 2026-07-01, so nothing has changed since. I read the statute in the enrolled P.L. 119-21 on govinfo, and the preamble in the full Federal Register text. Quotes are copied from those texts.
Legend: **V** = verified in primary legal text (statute, CFR, FR). **G** = official ED or HHS guidance that isn't binding law (press release, fact sheet, Dear Colleague letter, FSA training slides). **S** = secondary (CRS, press coverage).

## 6. Formulas for implementation

```
# RAP (34 CFR 685.209(b)(2),(f)(5),(g)(3),(h)(4),(o); HEA 455(q))
rate(AGI) = None if AGI <= 10000          # base = 120
          = ceil(AGI/10000)-1 percent for 10000 < AGI <= 100000   # (10k,20k]→1% ... (90k,100k]→9%
          = 10% if AGI > 100000
base = 120 if AGI <= 10000 else rate*AGI          # whole AGI, no marginal brackets
AGI  = MFJ ? joint AGI : own AGI                    # MFJ+separated/no access → own AGI
deps = IRC §152 dependents claimed on borrower's return (MFS: own return only)
pay  = max(10, base/12 - 50*deps)
if MFJ and spouse has eligible loans:              # eligible = Direct/FFEL except Parent PLUS & excepted consol
    pay = max(10, pay * own_PI_balance / (own_PI_balance + spouse_PI_balance))
pay  = min(pay, total_RAP_balance_PI)              # final payment
# each month, if payment is on time, in full, not advanced-due-date, AND for match: not in deferment/forbearance
interest_due   = interest accrued this month on all RAP loans
applied_int    = min(pay, interest_due)            # ED convention: current-month interest (see WE-6)
waived         = interest_due - applied_int        # waived only if on time
principal_paid = max(0, pay - interest_due - fees) # older outstanding interest is paid before principal
match          = max(0, min(50, pay) - principal_paid) if principal_paid < 50 else 0
# late, partial or missed month: no waiver, no match; interest accrues and is owed
forgive when qualifying_months >= 360 and last payment was under RAP

# IBR (685.209(b)(1),(b)(4),(f)(2)-(3),(g)(1))
DI   = max(0, AGI - 1.5*FPL(state, family_size, current HHS year))
calc = (0.10 if new_borrower else 0.15) * DI / 12
cap  = 10-yr standard payment on eligible balances & rates at IBR entry
pay  = min(calc, cap); if MFJ & spouse eligible loans: pay *= own_bal/(own_bal+spouse_bal)
if FFEL also: pay *= direct_bal/total_eligible_bal
pay  = 0 if pay < 5 else (10 if pay < 10 else pay)
forgive: 240 months (new) / 300 months (not new)

# Standard / Tiered Standard amortization (ED formula, 91 FR 34816)
M = P * (r/12) / (1 - (1 + r/12)^(-N))   ; N = 120 (10-yr), or Tiered: 120/180/240/300 by total Direct principal <25k/<50k/<100k/≥100k
M = max(M, 50) except final payment
```

## 7. Conflicts and open questions

1. **New loan → IBR loss: the regulation text is weaker than the statute.** §685.209(c)(3)(i) still says "any Direct Loan borrower may repay under the IBR plan", and (d)(5) restricts by *loan* date. The statute (§455(d)(1), (d)(7)(A)/(C)), the NPRM preamble, FSA slides and CRS all say a new loan moves **all** loans to Tiered Standard or RAP. Engine: follow the statute. Not settled: what ED does operationally to a borrower already in IBR who takes a new loan, and whether they are moved to Tiered Standard by default. Med.
2. **PAYE/ICR new enrollment before 2028.** ED's Jan 2025 final rule (90 FR 3695, effective 2026-07-01, implemented early) moved the "was repaying on July 1, 2024" cutoff to **July 1, 2027**. The RISE rule's text, also effective 2026-07-01 and what eCFR shows now, says **July 1, 2024**. I couldn't confirm which one ED applies today. Engine: don't recommend *new* PAYE/ICR enrollment; it ends 2028-06-30 anyway. Low-Med.
3. **Tiered Standard for borrowers with only pre-2026 loans.** ED's fact sheet (2026-06-09) says phased-out-plan borrowers can choose "Tiered Standard". §685.208(b)(8) and FSA slides limit it to borrowers with a post-2026-07-01 loan. Engine: follow the regulation. Med.
4. **SAVE default plan mapping and amortization.** For a SAVE borrower defaulted into "Standard", it's unknown how ED sets the remaining term (10 years from first entering repayment, excluding deferment and forbearance?). It's also unconfirmed whether old-loan borrowers go to 10-yr/consolidation Standard and new-loan borrowers to Tiered (my inference). Med-Low.
5. **Parent PLUS consolidation disbursed 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-30** ((c)(5)(iii) says ICR only through 2028-06-30) versus the statutory exclusion that makes it IBR-eligible after an ICR payment. It's unclear whether a $0 ICR month counts as "at least one payment" (FSA says "one full payment"). Low-Med.
6. **RAP qualifying months from deferment and forbearance.** The statute says months ending before **2025-07-04** (enactment); the regulation says before **2026-07-01**. It's also unclear whether SAVE litigation-forbearance months qualify; that forbearance isn't clearly one of the listed types. Engine: follow the regulation and flag. Med.
7. **Defaulted borrowers entering RAP directly.** The regulation text says defaulted loans are eligible for IBR and RAP. The preamble says RAP is available "after the default is resolved". ED paused involuntary collections in January 2026. Med-Low.
8. **Allocating multiple loans:** how the match and waiver are allocated across loans, and whether the 10-yr cap and Standard amortize per loan or on a weighted rate. The statute's RAP missed-recertification rule sums per-loan amortizations. Not specified elsewhere. Low.
9. **Rounding** of RAP and IBR payments (cents vs whole dollars) isn't specified. Use cents, half-up. Low.
10. **IBR $0 months toward RAP's 360.** The "minimum payment permitted" language suggests they count, but ED declined to say so explicitly. Med-Low.
11. **When ED switches to the new year's poverty guideline.** HHS effective 2026-01-13. ED's ICR notice applies 2026 figures to calculations from 2026-07-01 to 2027-06-30. The switch date for IBR calculations in Jan–Jun is unconfirmed. Med.
12. **Statutory drafting glitch:** §493C(e)(1) substitutes "10 percent" in subsection (a)(3)(B), but after P.L. 119-21 "15 percent" sits in the (a)(3) lead-in. The regulation implements 10% regardless. Note only.
