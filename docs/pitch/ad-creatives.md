# Ad Creatives — Student Loan Guardian

Three creatives, one per platform. Every creative:
- reads as **educational**, not a debt-settlement or debt-management pitch;
- labels any example figure **"estimate"**;
- includes, verbatim: **"Not affiliated with the Dept. of Education. Applying is free at StudentAid.gov."**
- makes **no personal-attribute claim** (nothing like "Your SAVE plan is ending" aimed at the viewer's situation);
- makes **no "$X off" / dollar-savings promise** in the ad copy itself.

---

## 1. Meta (Facebook/Instagram feed)

**Headline:** See your federal repayment options, side by side.

**Primary text:**
> Five federal repayment plans. One household. See how income, marital status, and kids change the estimate — for free, in under a minute.
>
> Not affiliated with the Dept. of Education. Applying is free at StudentAid.gov.

**CTA button:** Learn More

**Image/video:** Static product screenshot of the plan-comparison screen (sample/placeholder household, labeled "estimate" on-screen), neutral color palette, no distressed-person stock photography.

**Compliance notes (Meta Special Ad Category for credit/financial products):**
- Runs under the Special Ad Category — accept the broadened targeting: ages 18-65+, no gender targeting, 15-mile minimum radius, no ZIP-code targeting, no lookalike audiences.
- No specific dollar reduction claims ("cut your payment by $300"), no before/after balance comparisons, no lead form asking for the viewer's loan balance.
- No personal-situation callouts ("your SAVE plan is ending") — Meta treats this as implying the viewer's vulnerable financial status, which is banned regardless of accuracy.
- No claim to eliminate or reduce debt by a specific amount.

## 2. TikTok (in-feed video, 15s)

**On-screen hook (0-3s):** "Nobody tells you there are 5 different repayment plans."

**Voiceover/text overlay:**
> Five federal repayment plans exist. Most people only ever hear about one. This tool shows you an estimate for each — free to try, no login.
> Not affiliated with the Dept. of Education. Applying is free at StudentAid.gov.

**CTA:** Try it free

**Visual:** Screen-recording of the comparison UI changing as toggles are flipped (sample data, "estimate" labeled on screen) — no talking-head "borrower testimonial" framing, no implied before/after.

**Compliance notes (TikTok Housing/Employment/Credit policy):**
- Student loan content is classified as **credit** under TikTok's special-category policy: **no targeting by age, gender, ZIP code, marital status, or parental status** — even though family status (married, kids) is central to what our product actually computes, the ad itself must target broadly.
- No exaggerated results, no misleading before/after payment comparisons.
- Keep the visual generic (UI screen only) — no dramatized "crisis" framing of a borrower's situation.

---

## 3. Google Search

**Headline 1:** Compare Federal Repayment Plans
**Headline 2:** Free Plan Comparison Tool
**Headline 3:** Not a Government Site — Educational Estimate

**Description:**
> See an estimate for IBR, PAYE, RAP, and more, based on your income and household. Applying for any plan is free at StudentAid.gov. Not affiliated with the Dept. of Education.

**Display URL:** studentloanguardian.com/compare

**Compliance notes (Google Ads debt-services policy):**
- Google restricts debt-settlement/management advertising to approved nonprofit credit counseling agencies. Avoid any phrasing that reads as debt management — e.g., never "lower your student loan payment," only "compare your plan options" / "see an estimate."
- No seals, no "official," no "federal program update," no "forgiveness guaranteed," no government branding of any kind.
- Keep the "not affiliated" disclaimer in the ad copy itself, not just the landing page — it's what keeps this from reading as impersonating a government program.

---

## Platform/processor notes used to build these

- **Google Ads:** debt settlement/management ads limited to approved nonprofit credit counselors; avoid "lower your student loan payment" phrasing; no seals/"official"/"federal program update"/"forgiveness guaranteed."
- **Meta:** forced into the Special Ad Category (18-65+, no gender/ZIP targeting, 15-mile minimum radius, no lookalikes); banned: misleading claims about consolidation/forgiveness/refinancing, specific-dollar debt-reduction promises, implying vulnerable financial status.
- **TikTok:** student loans fall under its Housing/Employment/Credit policy — no targeting by age, gender, ZIP, marital, or parental status; no exaggerated or misleading before/after results.
- **Stripe (checkout, not the ad itself):** restricts "government services without authorization or value-add" — our checkout page and receipts must read clearly as paid software with a value-add (comparison, letters, audits), never as "we apply for you" or anything resembling a government fee.
- **Safe baseline used throughout:** an educational calculator framing; hypothetical examples explicitly labeled "estimate"; the exact line "Not affiliated with the Dept. of Education. Applying is free at StudentAid.gov"; price stated upfront, never hidden behind a lead form.
